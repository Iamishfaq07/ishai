# Age ratings (IARC questionnaire)

**What the page is.** Partner Center's Age ratings page is the International Age Rating Coalition (IARC)
questionnaire. You answer it once, click **Save and generate**, and the same rating is used for later updates.
Microsoft shares your publisher display name and email with IARC. Sources:
- <https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/publish/publish-your-app/msix/age-ratings>
- policy 11.11: "You are responsible for accurately completing the International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) rating
  questionnaire"

Microsoft doesn't publish the questions, so this sheet gives the honest answers by topic, not word for word.

**First question, the category:** choose the non-game one that fits best. In order of preference: utility,
productivity, reference or education. Don't choose social networking, a store, or entertainment. The later questions
depend on this choice.

| Topic | Answer | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Violence, fear, sexual content, nudity, crude humour, drugs, alcohol, tobacco, gambling | **No** | None of Orbit's own content has any of it. |
| Strong language | **No** | Orbit doesn't write it. The live transcript shows whatever the people on the call said, but that's the user's own conversation, not content Orbit provides. |
| Users can interact or communicate with each other | **No** | There's no user-to-user messaging. The support chat is with the Orbit team, and Saba is an AI. |
| Shares the user's location with other users | **No** | The country is used only to show prices in the right currency. |
| Digital purchases | **Yes** | Packs and plans, bought on the website, opened from the app. |
| Unrestricted internet access (a browser) | **No** | The app shows Orbit's own screens. Links open Orbit's website in the user's browser. |
| AI-generated content | Answer **truthfully** | Orbit's AI writes interview answers, questions and reviews from the user's documents and conversation, for career topics, under Orbit's instructions. It isn't an open-ended chatbot. |

**Expected result:** ESRB Everyone, PEGI 3, IARC 3+, with an "In-App Purchases" notice. For comparison, the Store's
catalog showed "Ai Interview Assistant" rated ESRB E, PEGI 3 and IARC 3+ with "In-App Purchases" (checked
2026-10-18).

**If it comes out Teen or 12+** (for example, because AI content counts as unpredictable), accept it. Don't change
answers to get a lower rating. Then check policy 11.11.3:

> if content might be appropriate for a higher age rating than its assigned rating, you must enable users to opt in
> [...] by using a content filter or by signing in with a pre-existing account

Orbit requires sign-in, which covers the second option. Orbit's privacy policy says it's for students and job
seekers, including people under 18.
