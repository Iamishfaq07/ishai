## Design Specificity Verdict
The pages are no longer interchangeable. The route line (stops filled, ringed or hollow) appears on Today, on the job page header, in the
Prepare job rows and in the tracker table, and it is the product's own mark of "where this job is". The serif titles
and the single route-green accent make the workbench recognisable without the logo. Detector: 0 findings on the changed
files (baseline: 18 warnings). The contrast failures from the baseline are fixed: placeholders are zinc-500 in light and
zinc-400 in dark, and the orange grade chip is gone.

## Priority Issues
- [P1] Prepare and My Career are still long scrolls of tool rows under the next step. Fix: show at most three contextual tools
  per hub and move the rest into the folded catalogue. Order the rows by the user's stage, not by category. ($impeccable distill)
- [P2] Error recovery outside Today: page-level ErrorNotes and disabled buttons without a visible reason. Fix: section-level
  neutral errors and reasons beside disabled actions. ($impeccable harden, $impeccable clarify)
- [P2] The tracker has no bulk or keyboard stage moves. Fix: multi-select in the table view and keyboard moves on board
  cards. ($impeccable adapt)
- [P2] Resume Studio, Offers and the specialist tools were not part of this redesign pass. Fix: run them through the same
  phase 2–4 rules (title face, no eyebrows or caps, None yet, quiet rows). ($impeccable polish)
