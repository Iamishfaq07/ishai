Cookie policy

Last updated 28 October 2026. Read this with the privacy policy.

What we set

  • Sign-in cookie. After you sign in, the site keeps an httpOnly cookie so the next page knows it's you. It is not readable by page scripts. Signing out, or deleting your account, ends it. This cookie is required for a signed-in account. There is no advertising cookie and no analytics cookie.
  • Google sign-in, for a few minutes. Choosing Continue with Google sets orbit_google, an httpOnly cookie that lasts 10 minutes. It only ties the return from Google to the browser that started it. It is deleted when Google sends you back.
  • This device only. The browser's own storage remembers the theme, whether you dismissed this notice, a support-chat token, answers you typed on this device before they're saved to your account, and where you first came from (the referring site, the link's campaign tags and the first page), which is sent once with your new account (see the privacy policy). Clearing site data removes them.

What we don't set

No ad network, no social tracker, and no cookie that follows you across other sites. Razorpay's payment window loads only when you choose to buy a pack, from Razorpay, and only for that payment. From outside India, Buy takes you to PayPal's own site to pay; any cookies there are PayPal's, under PayPal's policy. Crash reporting on the server is off unless it is configured, and it is not allowed to send your transcripts, documents or request bodies.

Your choice

You can keep using the public pages without an account. If you don't want the sign-in cookie, don't sign in, or sign out. You can also block cookies in the browser. The signed-in app will not stay signed in if you do.
