Orbit refund policy

Last updated 12 October 2026. For interview-minute and AI-credit packs.

The short version

  • There is no subscription. You pay once for a pack. Nothing renews, so there is nothing to cancel and no hidden fee.
  • Credits and minutes don't expire while your account is open.
  • Haven't used a pack? Ask within 7 days of it being added and we refund it in full to the original payment method.
  • A payment went wrong? We add the credits or refund you.

Packs you haven't used

If none of a pack's minutes or credits have been used, ask within 7 days of the credits being added. We refund the full amount the way you paid: back through Razorpay to the same UPI account, card or bank.

If any of that pack has been used, that pack is not refunded. Minutes and credits already used are not turned back into cash. A failed AI action is different: its credit comes back on its own.

Payment problems

  • You paid and the credits haven't arrived. Card, UPI, netbanking and wallet payments through Razorpay are added when the payment is confirmed. If they aren't there after a few minutes, write to us with the payment id or, for an older UPI payment checked by hand, the 12-digit UTR. We'll add the credits or refund you.
  • You paid twice, or paid the wrong amount. We refund the extra, or add what the amount covers if you'd rather.
  • We couldn't match a UPI payment. We'll say why on the Plan & credits page. If the money reached us, we refund it.

When Orbit gets it wrong

  • If an AI action (an import, a rewrite, a cover letter) fails, its credit comes back by itself.
  • If a live session or mock interview stops because of a fault on our side, tell us the session and when it happened. We'll put those minutes back.

What can't be refunded

  • The free welcome minutes and credit. They have no cash value.
  • Minutes and credits already used, apart from the cases above.

How to ask

Write from the contact page with the email on the account, how you paid, the date and the amount. We reply within 2 working days. An approved refund is sent within 7 working days.

Not happy with the answer? Write again from the contact page and say it's a complaint. We acknowledge it within 48 hours. This policy doesn't take away rights you have under Indian consumer law.
