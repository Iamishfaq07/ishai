Orbit terms of use

Last updated 12 October 2026. These terms cover the Orbit web app, the Orbit desktop app for Windows, and everything in them: interview practice and live help, Resume AI and Jobs AI.

Who we are

Orbit ("we") is run as an individual service, not a registered company. There is no GSTIN on these pages. You can reach us through the contact page for a question, a refund, a complaint, or a request to delete your data. The price you see before you pay is the full amount, in Indian rupees. Nothing is added at the last step.

Using Orbit

  • Orbit is for students and job seekers, including people under 18. Sign-up does not ask your age or date of birth.
  • One account is for one person. Keep your password to yourself. You're responsible for what is done with your account.
  • Don't try to claim the free welcome minutes or credit more than once, break the service, or get around its limits. We may suspend an account that does.

Use it where it's allowed

Orbit is built for practice, and for interviews, calls and work where notes or AI help are allowed. Some employers, colleges, exams and platforms don't allow them. Check the rules first. You are responsible for following them. Don't use Orbit:

  • in an exam, test or assessment that forbids outside help;
  • to record or transcribe people without telling them, where the law or the setting requires it;
  • to pretend to be someone else, or for anything illegal.

Orbit doesn't hide itself from other apps or from the people you talk to. The desktop app's optional privacy shield (off unless you turn it on) only asks Windows to keep its window out of screenshots and recordings; it isn't a way around anyone's rules.

AI suggestions can be wrong

Answers, resume rewrites, scores, job matches and fit checks are made by software and can be wrong or incomplete. Read them before you use them, and only say or send what is true about you. Orbit does not promise you an interview, a job, or a particular score. We don't publish reviews or user counts we can't show you in the product.

Your content

What you add (your resume, job posts, stories, transcripts) stays yours. You let us store and process it only to run Orbit for you, as the privacy policy explains. Don't upload other people's personal details without their permission. You can download your data, or delete your content or your whole account, in Settings.

Jobs from other sites

Jobs AI shows openings from companies' careers pages and job boards. We don't employ anyone, we can't check every employer, and we're not part of any hiring decision. A real employer never asks you to pay for a job, an interview or training. If a post asks for money, an OTP or a WhatsApp-only chat, don't go ahead, and tell us.

Credits, minutes and payments

  • Interview minutes pay for mock interviews and live sessions. AI credits pay for AI actions in Resume AI and Outreach. The price is shown in rupees before you pay.
  • You pay for a pack once, through Razorpay (UPI, a card, netbanking or a wallet). There is no subscription and nothing renews by itself.
  • Credits and minutes don't expire while your account is open. They can't be exchanged for cash or moved to another account.
  • If an AI action fails, its credit comes back.
  • Refunds follow the refund policy.

If Orbit closes, we'll say so on the site and leave Settings → Account able to download your data for at least 30 days after that notice. Unused paid credits would be refunded under the refund policy for packs that haven't been used. Orbit relies on other services (hosting, transcription, AI models, payments) and may sometimes be slow or unavailable.
