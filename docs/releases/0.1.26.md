## Orbit 0.1.26: Interview Atlas and Ask a mentor

- **Interview Atlas from the app:** Home now suggests the Interview Atlas. Search any company or role to see its
  interview process, practice questions, a day-by-day prep plan and its open jobs.
- **Ask a mentor:** after a mock interview, History has a "Share for feedback" button. It opens your report on the
  web, where you can send a private link to someone you trust. They can comment on each answer without an account.
  You choose whether your answers are included, and you can turn the link off at any time.
- **Security and stability:** updated Tauri and its dependencies, including the fixes to certificate checking.

Existing installs update automatically. Microsoft Store installs update through the Store.
