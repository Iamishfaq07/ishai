## Orbit 0.1.25: a new look, better answers, more ways to practise

- **A new look:** a redesigned app and a clearer live interview screen.
- **Better answers:**
  - Answers now cover the whole question, including the set-up and every part of a multi-part question.
  - Answers stay consistent through the interview and don't retell the same story twice.
  - Stronger answers to the classics: "tell me about yourself", "why us", strengths and weaknesses. "Do you have
    any questions for us?" now gives you smart questions to ask.
  - Answers use the company from your saved setup.
- **Reconnects continue the session:** if the connection drops, Orbit picks up where it left off.
- **Practice:**
  - Salary negotiation with an AI recruiter.
  - Mock interviewers that don't circle back to a topic, and difficulty levels that really differ.
  - Your story bank, suggested during setup.
- **On your phone:** follow the answers on your phone during a session.
- **Rate answers** with 👍 or 👎, and Orbit learns what you prefer. Add your own standing instructions to setup.
- **Resume maker:** setup shows how ATS software scores your resume, and opens the resume maker to improve it.
- Faster, smoother, and more secure.

Existing installs update automatically.
