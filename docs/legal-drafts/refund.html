Orbit refund policy

Draft, last updated 7 October 2026. For interview-minute and AI-credit packs bought with UPI.

The short version

  • There's no subscription. You pay once for a pack, nothing renews by itself, so there's nothing to cancel.
  • Credits and minutes never expire while your account is open, so you don't lose what you don't use this month.
  • Haven't used a pack? Ask within 7 days of it being added and we'll refund it in full.
  • Something went wrong with a payment? We'll fix it or refund it.

Packs you haven't used

If none of a pack's minutes or credits have been used, ask within 7 days of the credits being added to your account. We refund the full amount to the UPI account you paid from.

If you've used part of a pack, [owner: choose one: "we refund the unused part in proportion, within the same 7 days" or "the used part means the pack can't be refunded"].

Payment problems

  • You paid but the credits haven't arrived. Each UPI payment is checked by hand against its UTR (the 12-digit number your UPI app shows). If it's been more than a day, send us the UTR and we'll add the credits or refund you.
  • You paid twice, or paid the wrong amount. We refund the extra, or add what the amount covers if you'd rather.
  • We couldn't match your payment. We'll tell you why on the Plan & credits page. If the money reached us, we refund it.

When Orbit gets it wrong

  • If an AI action (an import, a rewrite, a cover letter) fails, its credit comes back by itself.
  • If a live session or mock interview breaks because of a fault on our side, tell us the session and when it happened, and we'll put the minutes back [owner to confirm].

What can't be refunded

  • The free welcome minutes and credit, which have no cash value.
  • Minutes and credits already used, apart from the cases above.

How to ask

Write to us from the contact page with your account's email, the UTR, the date and the amount. We reply within 2 working days, and an approved refund reaches your UPI account within 7 working days.

Not happy with the answer? Write to our grievance officer (details on the contact page). This policy doesn't take away rights you have under Indian consumer law.
